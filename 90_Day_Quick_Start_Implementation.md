**My Recommendation:** Choose **YouTube Creators** if you're not an expert in the above. Everyone understands YouTube, easy to demonstrate, overlaps perfectly with your Niche 1 audience.

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### DO THIS:
✅ Publish 2+ videos per week (consistency is key)
✅ Optimize based on data (what gets views? do more of that)
✅ Engage with comments daily (builds community)
✅ Use affiliate links naturally (help, don't push)
✅ Track metrics weekly (know your numbers)
✅ Improve video quality each month (editing, audio, etc.)

### DON'T DO THIS:
❌ Chase trending topics outside your niche
❌ Over-optimize too early (focus on quantity first)
❌ Neglect thumbnail/title quality
❌ Ignore comments and community
❌ Wait for "perfect" to publish
❌ Expect overnight success (compound growth takes time)
