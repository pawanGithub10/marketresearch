## PILLAR 1: CONTENT MARKETING STRATEGY

### Blog Post Content Plan (Per Calculator)

Each calculator gets **12-14 blog posts** (120-140 days = ~1 post per 10 days)

**Blog Post Categories & Word Count:**

1. **Core Educational Posts (3-4 posts, 2,500-3,500 words)**
   - What/Why/How guide (comprehensive)
   - Common mistakes + solutions
   - Advanced strategies + optimization tips
   - Example: "Complete Guide to Mortgage Affordability"

2. **Targeting Posts (4-5 posts, 1,500-2,000 words)**
   - By demographic (age, income, region)
   - By situation (new buyer, refinance, etc.)
   - By comparison (vs competitors, other products)
   - Example: "Unemployment Benefits by State Comparison"

3. **Seasonal Posts (2-3 posts, 1,200-1,500 words)**
   - Tax season optimizations
   - Holiday/end-of-year planning
   - Back-to-school financial planning
   - Example: "Last-Minute Tax Credits You Missed"

4. **Promotional Posts (2-3 posts, 800-1,200 words)**
   - Tool announcements
   - Case studies / success stories
   - Updates and new features
   - Example: "New State-by-State Unemployment Rates"

### Blog Publishing Schedule

**Month 1-2 (Days 1-60):**
- 6 posts (3x per week)
- Focus: Educational + foundational content
- SEO keyword targeting: Head terms + long-tail
- Internal linking: Link to calculator + related posts

**Month 3-4 (Days 61-130):**
- 8 posts (2x per week)
- Focus: Targeting specific demographics + seasonal
- SEO keyword targeting: Long-tail + voice search
- Link building: Promote for backlinks + citations

**Ongoing (After 130 days):**
- 2 posts per week maintenance
- Refresh old posts with updated data
- Respond to trending keywords
- Build content clusters

### Blog Post Template Structure

```markdown
# Main Title [Include Primary Keyword]

[Meta Description: 155-160 chars, include primary + secondary keyword]

## Executive Summary / Hook (100-200 words)
- Compelling opening (solve a problem immediately)
- What readers will learn
- Who it's for (demographic/situation)

## What: Core Concept Explanation (500-800 words)
- Define the topic clearly
- Break down components
- Use examples and comparisons
- Include data/statistics with sources

## Why: Context & Importance (400-600 words)
- Economic impact
- Real-world consequences
- Urgency/timing
- Global perspective (if applicable)
- Statistics from authoritative sources (Census, BLS, Federal Reserve, etc.)

## How: Practical Implementation (600-800 words)
- Step-by-step guide
- Example calculations
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Decision frameworks

## Advanced Strategies / Case Study (400-500 words)
- Optimization tips
- Real example with numbers
- Results/outcomes
- How to achieve similar results

## Key Takeaways (200-300 words)
- 5-7 bullet points
- Actionable items
- Easy to scan

## Call-to-Action (100-150 words)
- Mention calculator
- Link to calculator
- Secondary CTA (email signup, download)
- Social sharing

## References & Sources (50+ items)
- Hyperlinked to authoritative sources
- Mix of government, research, news
- Minimum 10 sources per post
```

## PILLAR 2: LINK BUILDING & SEO STRATEGY

### Target: 40-50 Quality Backlinks per Calculator (280-400 total)

**Backlink Profile Breakdown:**

```
25% High-Authority (DA 50+): 10-13 links per calculator
  - University/Government sites (.edu, .gov)
  - Major financial/medical publications
  - Industry associations
  - Examples: IRS.gov, Federal Reserve, CDC, ACOG

30% Mid-Authority (DA 30-49): 12-15 links per calculator
  - News outlets (CNBC, Forbes, Business Insider)
  - Reputable review sites
  - Industry blogs & publications
  - Examples: MarketWatch, NerdWallet, The Balance

25% Niche Relevant (DA 20-29): 10-13 links per calculator
  - Specialized blogs in your niche
  - Industry forums and communities
  - Relevant resource lists

20% Diverse Web (DA 10-19 + social): 8-10 links per calculator
  - Relevant social signals (Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn)
  - Community participation
  - Branded mentions
```

### Link Building Tactics (Ranked by ROI)

**Tier 1: High-ROI, Quick Wins (Complete in Month 1)**

1. **HARO (Help A Reporter Out)** - $0 cost
   - Monitor journalist queries related to your niches
   - Respond with expertise + calculator link
   - Target: 2-4 links in first month
   - Timeline: 1-2 weeks per link

2. **Resource Page Requests**
   - Find relevant resource pages (100+ financial/health sites)
   - Request inclusion with your calculator
   - Example: "Best Tax Calculators" + list 5 best + yours
   - Success rate: 15-25%
   - Target: 5-8 links in month 1

3. **Local/Industry Partnerships**
   - Tax preparation companies (H&R Block, TurboTax, local CPAs)
   - Insurance brokers (auto, home, health)
   - OB-GYNs, fertility clinics, pregnancy centers
   - Loan officers, mortgage brokers
   - State Unemployment agencies (some link to tools)
   - Freelancer communities (design/dev networks)
   - Target: 3-5 partnerships

**Tier 2: Medium-ROI, Builds Authority (Month 2-3)**

4. **Guest Posting**
   - Write 1,500-2,000 word guest posts for relevant blogs
   - Include calculator link in bio/content
   - Target: Medium-authority blogs (DA 30+)
   - Create 3-5 guest posts = 3-5 links + traffic

5. **Broken Link Building**
   - Find broken links on high-authority sites
   - Replace with your content
   - Email webmaster with replacement
   - Target: 2-4 links per calculator

6. **Infographic Outreach**
   - Create simple infographic from calculator data
   - Reach out to 50+ relevant sites
   - Success rate: 10-15%
   - Target: 5-8 links per calculator

**Tier 3: Long-Tail, Relationship Building (Month 3-4)**

7. **Directory Submissions**
   - High-authority directories (DMOZ, Yahoo Directory alternatives)
   - Industry directories (tax, health, financial)
   - Local directories (Better Business Bureau)
   - Free submissions: Google My Business, Yelp, Trustpilot
   - Target: 5-10 diverse citations

8. **Influencer/Authority Outreach**
   - Identify 100+ relevant influencers in each niche
   - Personalized outreach (NOT automated)
   - Offer exclusive access / beta features
   - Success rate: 5-10%
   - Target: 3-5 mentions per calculator

9. **Press Release Distribution**
   - Write newsworthy press release per calculator
   - Distribute via PRNewswire / EIN Presswire
   - Target: Local news + industry press
   - Cost: $150-300 per release
   - Expected links: 2-5 per release

### Link Building Schedule

**Month 1 (Days 1-30):**
- Week 1-2: Set up HARO, identify resource pages, start outreach
- Week 3-4: Monitor HARO queries, handle responses, send resource page requests
- Target: 10-15 total links across all calculators

**Month 2 (Days 31-60):**
- Week 5-6: Guest posts (write 2-3), broken link building
- Week 7-8: Create 2-3 infographics, start influencer outreach
- Target: 15-20 new links

**Month 3 (Days 61-90):**
- Week 9-10: Press releases, directory submissions
- Week 11-12: Follow-up outreach, relationship building
- Target: 10-15 new links

**Month 4+ (Ongoing):**
- Maintain 2-3 links per week through relationship building
- Refresh top content to attract new links
- Monitor backlink profile for opportunities

### Technical SEO Checklist

- [ ] Site speed optimization (Core Web Vitals < 2.5s LCP)
- [ ] Mobile responsiveness (100% mobile-friendly)
- [ ] XML sitemap (submitted to Google Search Console)
- [ ] Robots.txt optimization (allow all crawlers)
- [ ] Schema.org markup (Calculator, Organization, BreadcrumbList)
- [ ] Internal linking structure (150+ internal links across 8 calculators)
- [ ] Heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3 properly structured)
- [ ] Image optimization (ALT text, WebP format, compression)
- [ ] Page load speed (images lazy-loaded, CSS minified)
- [ ] 404 error monitoring (regular crawl checks)

## PILLAR 3: PAID ADVERTISING STRATEGY

### Google Ads (Search + Display)

**Budget Allocation:** $1,500-$2,000/month (scales with performance)

**Monthly Budget by Calculator:**
- Income Tax: $250 (broad audience, high intent)
- Mortgage: $200 (broad audience, high value)
- Pregnancy: $150 (niche audience, seasonal)
- Car Loan: $200 (broad audience, high intent)
- Unemployment: $300 (high intent, immediate need)
- Ovulation/Fertility: $150 (niche audience, seasonal)
- Freelancer Rate: $100 (lower search volume)
- EITC: $300 (high intent, seasonal tax season)

**Google Ads Campaign Structure:**

```
Campaign 1: Branded Keywords
- Keywords: [Calculator Name] Calculator, [Brand Name]
- Bid: $2-5 CPC
- Goal: Capture direct searchers, prevent competitor ads

Campaign 2: Non-Branded Head Keywords
- Keywords: "income tax calculator", "mortgage calculator", etc.
- Bid: $3-8 CPC (varies by keyword)
- Goal: Attract intent-based searchers

Campaign 3: Long-Tail Keywords
- Keywords: "2024 income tax calculator for self-employed", etc.
- Bid: $1-3 CPC (lower volume, lower cost)
- Goal: High-quality, specific intent traffic

Campaign 4: Comparison Keywords
- Keywords: "[Calculator] vs [Competitor]", "best [calculator]"
- Bid: $2-5 CPC
- Goal: Capture consideration-stage searchers

Campaign 5: Local Keywords (if location-specific)
- Keywords: "[Calculator] [state/city]"
- Bid: $2-4 CPC
- Goal: Local audience (state unemployment, state tax)
```

**Expected Google Ads Performance:**

- Average CPC (Cost Per Click): $2-5 across all keywords
- Expected CTR (Click-Through Rate): 3-8% (depends on position)
- Conversion Rate (to calculator use): 30-50% (high-intent traffic)
- Cost Per Lead: $8-20 (varies by calculator)
- Daily Budget: $50-70/calculator in month 1, scale to $100-150 based on ROAS
