How Debt Consolidation Works

The Concept

Debt consolidation combines multiple high-interest debts into a single loan with a potentially lower interest rate. Instead of paying $400 to credit card A, $250 to credit card B, and $180 to a personal loan, you make one $650 payment to your consolidation loan.

The benefit: lower overall interest rates mean you save money over time and simplify your finances. The trade-off: you may extend your payoff timeline if you're not careful.

The Formula

Monthly Payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:

P = Total Principal (sum of all debts)

r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)

n = Total number of payments (months)

Global Usage Notes

  • USA: Debt consolidation is common. Personal loans from banks, LendingClub, or Discover are popular. Average consolidation rate: 7-14% depending on credit score.
  • India: Banks and NBFCs (Non-Banking Financial Companies) offer personal loans for consolidation. Rates: 10-16%. GST applies on interest.
  • UK: Called a "debt consolidation loan." Secured or unsecured options. Interest rates: 3-10% depending on credit.
  • Canada: Similar to USA. Credit unions and banks offer low rates (5-10%) for good credit. Debt consolidation is regulated by provincial laws.

This directly calculates the monthly payment. E2=rate, F2=months, G2=principal.

Google Sheets Formulas

Google Sheets syntax is identical to Excel for these functions:

  2. Enter your debt amounts and rates in cells A2, B2, C2, D2, etc.

  4. In G2, enter: =A2+C2
  5. In H2, enter: =PMT(E2/12/100, F2, -G2)

Python Implementation

Use this Python function to calculate debt consolidation in your app or script.

def calculate_debt_consolidation(debts, consolidation_rate, months):
    """
    Calculate debt consolidation savings.

    Args:
        debts: List of tuples [(amount, rate), (amount, rate), ...]
        consolidation_rate: Annual interest rate (%) for consolidation loan
        months: Loan term in months

    Returns:
        dict with monthly_payment, total_paid, interest_savings
    """
    total_debt = sum(d[0] for d in debts)

    # Current total interest calculation
    current_total = 0
    for amount, rate in debts:
        # Simple interest: Principal * Rate * Time
        current_total += amount * (rate / 100) * (months / 12)

    # Consolidation calculation
    r = consolidation_rate / 12 / 100
    monthly_payment = total_debt * (r * (1 + r)**months) / ((1 + r)**months - 1)
    total_paid = monthly_payment * months
    interest_paid = total_paid - total_debt
    savings = current_total - interest_paid

    return {
        'monthly_payment': round(monthly_payment, 2),
        'total_paid': round(total_paid, 2),
        'interest_savings': round(max(savings, 0), 2)
    }

# Example usage:
debts = [(5000, 18), (3000, 12), (2000, 8)]
result = calculate_debt_consolidation(debts, 9, 60)
print(f"Monthly Payment: ${result['monthly_payment']}")
print(f"Interest Savings: ${result['interest_savings']}")

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is debt consolidation?

Debt consolidation is the process of combining multiple debts (credit cards, personal loans, medical bills, etc.) into a single new loan. This simplifies your finances and can lower your overall interest rate, saving you money.

Can I consolidate credit cards?

Yes, absolutely. In fact, credit cards often have the highest interest rates (15-25%), making them prime candidates for consolidation. Many people consolidate high-interest credit cards into a personal loan at 7-14%.

How does consolidation affect my credit score?

In the short term, a hard credit inquiry may slightly lower your score (5-10 points). However, consolidating reduces your credit utilization and simplifies your credit profile, which typically improves your score over 3-6 months.

Is consolidation different in India vs. USA?

USA: Common personal loan consolidation through banks and fintech lenders. No GST on interest. India: Banks and NBFCs offer personal loans for consolidation. GST (18%) applies on interest. Rates are typically 10-18% in India vs. 7-14% in USA.

What's the difference between consolidation and debt settlement?

Consolidation: Borrow a new loan to pay off all debts. You pay back 100% of the original amount. Settlement: Negotiate with creditors to pay less than owed (e.g., 60% of balance). Settlement damages your credit more.

Should I consolidate if my new payment is similar to my current one?

No, unless the consolidation loan has significantly lower interest (2-3% less). If your monthly payment stays the same but you pay less interest, that's a win. Use this calculator to compare the total interest paid.

Is this calculator a substitute for professional financial advice?

No. This calculator is a helpful educational tool to estimate savings. Always consult with a financial advisor, credit counselor, or loan officer before making consolidation decisions. Actual rates and terms depend on your credit score, income, and lender.
